# Applying for Membership

Check the types of membership and their eligibility [here](eligibility.md)

## Steps
1. [Download](membershipforms.md) the corresponding application form in fillable PDF format
2. Fill the application form in onw of the following ways:
    * Fill the PDF file using software such as [Adobe Acrobat](https://get.adobe.com/reader/otherversions), [BentoPDF](https://bentopdf.com/form-filler.html) or your favourite PDF form filling software
    * Download and print the application form and affix your signature and a recent photograph
3. Scanned image of your postgraduate degree certificate
4. Scanned image of your Aadhar card
5. Email the application form along with copies of degree certificate, Aadhar card and payment proof to sea.india.2025@outlook.com

## Checklist for filling the form
* Photograph (if filling the print version of the form) or scanned image of your photograph (if filling the form electronically)
* Scanned copy of the filled and signed printed application form with photograph and signature or electronically filled application form
* Scanned copy of your postgraduate degree certificate
* Scanned image or PDF copy of your Aadhar card
* Scanned copy of your proof of payment
